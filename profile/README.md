## About us

We study how metabolic interactions between T cells, macrophages, and tumor cells determine immune function and tumor progression. To address these questions, we use **multiplexed ion beam imaging (MIBI)** — a proteomic imaging technology that simultaneously quantifies 40+ proteins with spatial resolution directly in human tissue.

Our work spans wet-lab spatial proteomics and the computational methods needed to make sense of it: image processing, cell segmentation, panel-adaptive representation learning, and multicellular program discovery.
